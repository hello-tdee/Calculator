What Is TDEE?

Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) is the estimated number of calories your body uses in a full day. It includes the energy needed for basic body functions, digestion, everyday movement and exercise.

How Does This TDEE Calculator Work?

This calculator estimates Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. It then multiplies BMR by an activity factor to estimate total daily energy expenditure. The result can help you understand your approximate daily energy needs.

What Is BMR?

Basal Metabolic Rate is an estimate of the energy your body uses at rest to support essential functions. Your TDEE is higher than your BMR because it also accounts for physical activity and other daily energy use.

How Many Calories Do You Need?

Daily calorie requirements vary according to age, body size, activity, health and other individual factors. The calculator provides an initial estimate for maintaining, gradually losing or gaining weight. Real-world needs may differ, so interpret the results as a starting point rather than an exact prescription.

Is a TDEE Calculator Accurate?

A TDEE calculator provides an estimate rather than a direct measurement of metabolism. Tracking changes over time may help you understand your needs, but consult a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian for personalised guidance.
