What Is TDEE?

Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) is the estimated number of calories your body uses in a full day. It includes energy used at rest, digestion, daily movement and exercise.

How does this TDEE calculator work?

This calculator estimates your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, then multiplies it by an activity factor to estimate daily calorie expenditure.

  • BMR: Calories your body needs for basic functions at rest.
  • TDEE: Estimated calories burned throughout the day.
  • Maintenance: An estimated intake for maintaining weight over time.

How many calories should you eat?

Your calorie needs depend on your body size, age, activity and goals. The result can be a starting estimate. Track your progress over several weeks and adjust sensibly rather than relying on a single calculation.

Is this calculator accurate?

It provides an estimate, not an exact measurement. Individual metabolism, health, medications and changes in activity can affect actual energy needs.
