What Is TDEE?

Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) is an estimate of the energy your body burns over a full day. It includes the energy used at rest, digestion, everyday movement and exercise.

Your estimated maintenance calories are the calories you may need to maintain your current weight over time. Your actual needs depend on factors such as body composition, health, sleep and activity.

How Should You Use Your Result?

Your TDEE is a starting estimate, not an exact measurement. Track your results over several weeks and consider adjusting your intake gradually based on your needs and progress.

Avoid extreme calorie restriction. If you are under 18, pregnant, breastfeeding, have a medical condition or have a history of disordered eating, ask a qualified healthcare professional for individualized advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What does TDEE stand for?

TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure, an estimate of the total calories your body burns in a day.

2. What is BMR?

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) estimates the energy your body uses at rest to support essential functions.

3. How is TDEE calculated?

This calculator estimates BMR using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, then multiplies it by an activity factor.

4. Which activity level should I choose?

Choose the option that most closely reflects your usual exercise and daily movement. Activity categories are approximate.

5. Are TDEE calculator results exact?

No. The result is an estimate. Your actual energy expenditure can differ due to individual and lifestyle factors.

6. What are maintenance calories?

Maintenance calories are the estimated daily calories needed to keep your weight relatively stable over time.

7. How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

Weight loss usually requires an energy deficit over time. The calculator shows an illustrative 300-calorie reduction, not a personalized prescription.

8. How many calories should I eat to gain weight?

Weight gain generally requires an energy surplus over time. The calculator shows an illustrative 300-calorie increase.

9. Should I recalculate my TDEE?

Recalculate when your weight, activity routine or other relevant circumstances change significantly.

10. Can I use this calculator on my phone?

Yes. The page layout adjusts for mobile screens and desktop browsers.

11. Does this calculator store my personal details?

The calculator runs in your browser and this code does not send your entered measurements to a server.

12. Is this calculator a substitute for medical advice?

No. It is an educational tool and does not diagnose, treat or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
